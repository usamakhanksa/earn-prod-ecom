# ReplyRight — Start Here Guide

## What this actually is
A subscription tool that writes review replies for local businesses. Not passive income.
Not "daily earnings." A small B2B SaaS product that makes real money only if you sell it
to real business owners, one conversation at a time.

## The math, unrounded
- Price: $15/month (sweet spot for SMB tools — cheap enough to not need approval,
  expensive enough to cover Anthropic API + Stripe fees with margin)
- To hit $1,500/month (~$50/day averaged) you need **100 paying customers**
- Cold outreach realistically converts 2–5% (message → paying customer)
- That means **2,000–5,000 outbound contacts** before you're near 100 customers
- Timeline: 3–6 months of daily outreach, not automatic, not overnight

## Costs that eat your margin (don't skip this)
- Anthropic API: pennies per reply generated, but it's not zero — track it
- Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Supabase: free tier covers you until a few thousand users, then ~$25/mo
- Vercel: free tier covers you until real traffic, then usage-based
- Your time: unpaid, and this is the actual cost that kills most solo projects

## Distribution plan (this matters more than the code)
1. **Week 1–2 (build):** Ship the MVP using the build prompt below.
2. **Week 3 onward (sell):** Every single day, find 20–30 local businesses on
   Google Maps in a category with lots of reviews (dentists, med spas, restaurants,
   auto shops, salons). Get their email or Instagram DM. Send a short, specific
   message: "I noticed [Business Name] has 40 unreplied reviews on Google — I built
   a tool that drafts professional replies in 10 seconds, want to try it free?"
3. Attach a live demo link, not a pitch deck. Let them try it before they pay.
4. Track everything in a spreadsheet: contacted, replied, trial, paid. If your
   contact→paid conversion is under 1% after 200 messages, the message or the
   product is wrong — fix that before scaling volume.
5. Do NOT wait on SEO, Product Hunt, or "going viral" as your primary plan.
   Those are lottery tickets. Outbound is the only channel you control.

## Reality checkpoints (be honest with yourself here)
- If after 500 real outbound contacts you have 0 paying customers, the idea or
  your outreach is broken — diagnose before building more features.
- If you get 10 paying customers, you've validated something real. Most people
  who start this never get past customer #1. Getting there is the actual win.
- Nobody will "discover" this app organically in month one. If you're not doing
  outreach every day, you're not running a business, you're hosting a hobby.

## Alternate ideas considered and why I didn't pick them
- **Consumer AI wrapper (resume/cover letter/chatbot):** oversaturated, competing
  against free ChatGPT, near-zero willingness to pay.
- **Ad-supported content or directory site:** real revenue needs real SEO traffic,
  which takes 6–12+ months minimum. Too slow if you want revenue this year.
- **Chrome extension niche price tracker:** viable, but distribution depends on
  Chrome Web Store search ranking, which you don't control. SMB outreach is more
  reliable because you can literally talk to the buyer.
