# From Sign-Up to Loyalty Loop: A Strategic Playbook for the OmniSell Consumer Points Economy

This report provides a comprehensive guide for the successful implementation and strategic management of the OmniSell OS consumer points and wallet system. Grounded exclusively in the specifications outlined in the `POINTS-EXTENSION.md` document, this analysis covers five critical domains: business registration and onboarding, application programming interface (API) architecture, marketing targeting, investment strategy, and customer acquisition. The framework detailed herein is designed to operationalize the platform's new loyalty economy, ensuring technical robustness, strategic alignment, and a superior user experience. The entire system is predicated on a multi-tenant architecture where every piece of consumer-related data, from wallets to transactions, is strictly scoped to a tenant, enforcing isolation and security at the data layer [[7](https://www.lyrid.io/post/single-tenant-vs-multi-tenant-architecture-how-to-treat-your-app-and-wallet-the-best), [252](https://workos.com/blog/tenant-isolation-in-multi-tenant-systems)]. This foundational principle dictates every aspect of the following guidance.

## Tenant Onboarding and Consumer Enrollment Framework

The onboarding process for the OmniSell OS points system must be understood as a bifurcated journey, encompassing two distinct but interconnected flows: the registration of a new tenant, who operates the platform as a creator, and the enrollment of an end-consumer, who interacts with that tenant's store. The system's design emphasizes a unified user identity, allowing a single account to seamlessly switch between these modes, a feature that has profound implications for both the onboarding experience and long-term user engagement [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The process for establishing a new tenant is not significantly altered by the introduction of the points system; rather, it is augmented with automated provisioning of the necessary loyalty engine components. For a new tenant, the existing creator onboarding flow detailed in `prompt.md` remains the primary mechanism [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. Upon successful creation of the tenant account, the system automatically initializes a complete set of tenant-scoped configurations required for the points economy to function. This includes creating a corresponding entry in the `Wallet` table for the tenant itself, seeding default `PointEarningRule` entries—for instance, a baseline rule for `video_watch` action— and generating a `TenantPointSettings` record [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The `TenantPointSettings` table is the central hub for all business logic related to the loyalty program, defining the crucial parameters such as the points-to-currency exchange rate (`pointsPerCurrencyMinor`), minimum redemption thresholds (`minRedeemPoints`), maximum share of order payable by points (`maxRedeemSharePct`), and whether redemptions are enabled at all [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Therefore, the initial setup for a new tenant involves pre-configuring these settings, likely using default values established during the sign-up process or provided through an initial admin dashboard. Once this is complete, the tenant operator can immediately begin managing the loyalty program through the dedicated administrative interfaces, focusing on curating content within the `VideoContent` table, fine-tuning earning rules, and adjusting redemption policies [[22](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/loyalty-api/overview), [24](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/atlas.en-us.loyalty.meta/loyalty/loyalty_api_overview.htm)].

In parallel, the onboarding of an end-consumer is an automatic and frictionless process. When a user signs up for an OmniSell account, they are implicitly enrolled as a member of the consumer ecosystem. There is no separate "join the loyalty program" button or form; enrollment is intrinsic to the creation of a standard user account [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. The system automatically creates a `Wallet` for the new user, scoped to the relevant tenant, but with an initial balance of zero points [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The onboarding journey begins immediately with a curated introductory experience designed to drive the first point-earning action. A key part of this process is prompting the user to watch an introductory video, prominently labeled with a call-to-action such as "Watch this and earn your first 50 points" [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. This transforms the passive act of viewing promotional content into an active, rewarding engagement that immediately demonstrates the value proposition of the loyalty system. Furthermore, certain features are gated behind specific conditions to guide the user's progression. For example, the ability to redeem points for discounts on products is initially disabled until the user's earned points balance successfully clears the `minRedeemPoints` floor configured in `TenantPointSettings` [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [108](https://developer.eagleeye.com/eagleeye-developer/docs/segment)]. This ensures that users understand the threshold required to unlock the full benefits of the system.

A cornerstone of the OmniSell OS points system is the unification of the creator and consumer roles under a single `User` entity [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. A user does not need to create separate accounts for selling products and for shopping; instead, they possess one profile with the ability to switch between Creator Mode and Consumer Mode at runtime [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This identity fusion is a powerful strategic advantage. It fosters a more integrated community where creators are also consumers, potentially increasing platform stickiness and reducing churn. This dual-role capability allows a user to seamlessly transition from creating content and products to browsing and purchasing them as a shopper, deepening their immersion in the ecosystem. The front-end applications reflect this with mode-switching UI elements, such as a top-bar toggle on web or a dedicated tab set on mobile, which persists the user's choice and correctly labels the active mode [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This unified identity extends to the wallet and transaction history, which serves both modes, providing a holistic view of a user's activity across the platform. This approach contrasts with traditional e-commerce platforms where seller and buyer accounts are often siloed, and it positions OmniSell as a more cohesive social and commercial network.

## API Architecture and Technical Implementation Blueprint

The technical architecture of the OmniSell OS points system is meticulously defined in `POINTS-EXTENSION.md`, providing a clear blueprint for development that prioritizes data integrity, security, and horizontal scalability. At its core, the system is built upon a set of new tenant-scoped tables defined in the Prisma schema, which extend the existing OmniSell data model without altering its base entities [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The central table, `Wallet`, contains the cached balance for a given `(tenantId, userId)` pair, along with a `version` integer for optimistic locking [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. All other tables, including `PointTransaction`, `PointEarningRule`, `VideoContent`, `VideoWatch`, and `ProductPurchaseWithPoints`, are explicitly tied to a `tenantId`, ensuring strict data isolation [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Every read operation must enforce this tenant scope at the repository level, with Row-Level Security (RLS) serving as a critical secondary line of defense against cross-tenant data leakage. A mandatory cross-tenant negative test ensures this isolation is robustly enforced [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)].

The `PointTransaction` table is the authoritative source for all changes to a user's point balance. Its structure is rich with metadata, including a `type` (EARN, SPEND, ADJUST, EXPIRY), a `source` (e.g., "video_watch"), a `sourceId` linking back to the originating event (like a `VideoWatch.id`), and a `status` (PENDING, VALIDATED, REVERSED) [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. Crucially, the specification mandates immutability once a transaction is validated; corrections must be made by creating a new `ADJUST` transaction with a mandatory reason code, preserving a complete and auditable trail of all financial activities [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The `Wallet.balance` field is not directly updated. Instead, it is a derived value—a cached projection computed by summing all `VALIDATED` transactions for a given wallet [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. To prevent race conditions during updates, writes to the wallet are protected by optimistic locking using the `version` column. Before committing any change, the system verifies that the cached balance matches the derived balance. If they do not match, the write fails closed, and a reconciliation alert is triggered, ensuring the system never silently corrects a discrepancy [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This rigorous validation is complemented by a nightly job that recomputes balances for touched wallets, providing an additional layer of auditability [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)].

The API provides a set of well-defined endpoints that adhere to the platform's established conventions, including cursor-based pagination, use of `Idempotency-Key` on POST requests, and standardized `problem+json` error responses [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The primary endpoints for the consumer-facing wallet and earning functionality include `GET /v1/wallet` to retrieve the current balance and transaction summary, and `POST /v1/wallet/earn/video-watch` as a client-facing convenience endpoint for initiating the video-earning process [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Under the hood, this triggers a more granular internal pipeline consisting of `POST /v1/video-watches` to start a session, `POST /v1/video-watches/:id/heartbeat` to log progress, and `POST /v1/video-watches/:id/complete` to finalize the watch and trigger award processing [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. For redemption, a two-step process is implemented to decouple preview from commitment. First, `POST /v1/wallet/redeem` accepts a product ID and points to use, validating the request against all rules (balance, floor, share cap) and returning a preview of the discount without deducting any points [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Second, upon order confirmation, `POST /v1/wallet/redeem/confirm` performs the final deduction (`SPEND` transaction), applies the discount, and confirms the redemption, requiring an `Idempotency-Key` to prevent accidental double-redemption [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)].

To support high concurrency and ensure a responsive user experience, the architecture employs asynchronous processing and external caching layers. Live video watch sessions, particularly those in the `WATCHING` state, are stored in a Redis instance with Time-To-Live (TTL) keys [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This allows the application to scale horizontally across multiple API server instances, as the watch state is not tied to a single server's memory [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The computationally intensive task of validating completed watches—checking for fraud, enforcing daily caps and cooldowns, updating the wallet, and logging the transaction—is offloaded to a background worker using BullMQ [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This worker is idempotent, meaning it will not award points twice even if the completion event is received multiple times, and it is equipped with a Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) to handle any processing failures gracefully [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Finally, the system's global behavior is controlled by a set of environment variables documented in `README.md` and `.env.example`. These include `POINTS_VIDEO_MIN_WATCH_SECONDS` (default 30), `POINTS_DAILY_EARNING_CAP` (default 500), `POINTS_FRAUD_DETECTION_ENABLED` (default true), `POINTS_HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_SECONDS` (default 5), and `POINTS_MAX_WATCHES_PER_DAY` (default 10) [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. These values provide the foundational constants for the earning engine and can be overridden per-environment to tune performance and security.

## Data-Driven Marketing Segmentation and Targeting

The OmniSell OS points system generates a rich stream of behavioral data that enables sophisticated marketing segmentation, moving beyond static demographics to create dynamic, actionable consumer profiles based on actual engagement with the platform [[30](https://scal-e.com/how-to-segment-customers-for-a-customised-loyalty-programme/), [34](https://www.comarch.com/trade-and-services/loyalty-marketing/blog/customer-segmentation-in-loyalty-programs-continuing-the-conversation/)]. The dual nature of the platform, where users can simultaneously act as creators and consumers, provides an even deeper layer of insight into their motivations and behaviors [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. By analyzing how users interact with the points economy—from watching videos to redeeming discounts—it is possible to identify distinct personas that can be targeted with highly personalized campaigns and product recommendations. This approach transforms marketing from a broad-guest strategy into a precise, data-driven discipline that enhances customer loyalty and drives revenue [[25](https://www.openloyalty.io/insider/loyalty-segmentation-guide), [31](https://www.snipp.com/blog/loyalty-segmentation-tips)].

Based on the documented mechanics of the points system, several key consumer personas emerge. The **Engaged Learner** persona consists of users who consistently participate in the `video_watch` action to accumulate points. They are highly engaged with the educational and promotional content supplied by tenants (creators) and represent a valuable audience for content-based marketing. For this segment, marketing efforts should focus on delivering personalized video feeds and highlighting new `VideoContent` that aligns with their viewing history. The **Value-Driven Shopper** persona is motivated primarily by the prospect of saving money. Their engagement is triggered by the potential for a discount, making them highly sensitive to the parameters set in `TenantPointSettings`, such as the points-to-currency conversion rate and the maximum percentage of an order they can pay with points [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Campaigns targeting this group should emphasize the tangible value of their point balance and highlight products that offer the best "bang for their buck" when paid for with points. A third, higher-value segment is the **Hybrid Creator-Shopper**. These are users who actively participate in both modes, perhaps watching their own products' videos or those of peers while also browsing and purchasing items [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This indicates a deep level of platform adoption and brand affinity, making them ideal candidates for community-building initiatives, early access to new features, and premium loyalty tiers.

The ability to target these segments effectively relies on the structured data captured throughout the user journey. Every `VideoWatch` records which video was watched, for how long, and the outcome (credited, fraud suspected, etc.). Every `PointTransaction` logs the source of the points. This data, combined with traditional e-commerce data from orders, allows for the creation of detailed user profiles [[30](https://scal-e.com/how-to-segment-customers-for-a-customised-loyalty-programme/)]. For instance, a user who frequently watches videos about sustainable fashion and then redeems points for eco-friendly apparel has a clear affinity that can be reinforced with targeted communications. Audience segmentation is the practice of dividing a large customer base into smaller, more homogeneous groups, and the data generated by the points system provides a powerful foundation for this process [[28](https://www.yotpo.com/blog/loyalty-segmentation/), [32](https://aerospike.com/blog/real-time-audience-segmentation/)]. By identifying patterns in behavior, businesses can move away from generic mass marketing and toward personalization at scale, which is proven to increase engagement and sales [[27](https://manago.ai/blog/customer-loyalty-program)]. For example, understanding that a segment of users is very active but doesn't use competitors' services (the 'Frequent, Loyal' segment) allows for hyper-targeted offers designed to deepen that loyalty [[26](https://medium.com/@onecaseman/loyalty-marketing-part-i-strategies-and-segments-bbd326d9b130)].

Furthermore, the `TenantPointSettings` table introduces a layer of tenant-specific configuration that must be considered in marketing strategy. While the underlying mechanics are consistent, the perceived value of the loyalty program can vary significantly between tenants depending on their chosen exchange rates, redemption floors, and caps [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. A marketing campaign run by one tenant cannot assume the same appeal for another. Therefore, the system must allow tenant operators to define their own rules, and the marketing analytics layer must be capable of slicing and dicing performance data by these tenant-level configurations. This enables each tenant to optimize their loyalty program for their specific audience and business goals. The `featureslist.md` file provides a priority matrix (`Pri`) for various features, which can guide the sequencing of data collection and reporting capabilities [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. For example, P0 features like `C-1` (Consumer wallet) and `C-2` (Points from videos) should have immediate and robust analytics attached, while P2 features like `C-8` (Referral earning) would require more advanced attribution models once implemented. This phased approach ensures that the most critical data for understanding user behavior and driving business value is available from day one. Ultimately, the goal is to build a Customer Data Platform (CDP) that ingests this behavioral data, enriches it with other signals, and provides actionable insights to marketers through dashboards and APIs, enabling them to execute the strategies outlined in guides on loyalty segmentation and personalization [[25](https://www.openloyalty.io/insider/loyalty-segmentation-guide), [108](https://developer.eagleeye.com/eagleeye-developer/docs/segment)].

## Investment Planning and Unit Economic Analysis

A disciplined investment strategy is critical for the successful launch and scaling of the OmniSell OS points system. The guidance derived from the project documentation suggests a phased investment plan that prioritizes building a stable and secure core before expanding into more complex, value-add features. This approach minimizes risk and ensures that the fundamental value proposition is robust before significant capital is allocated to downstream integrations and user experience enhancements. The unit economic analysis reveals that the primary driver of ROI is not the direct arbitrage of points for currency but the long-term increase in customer lifetime value (CLV) generated by fostering deeper user engagement and loyalty [[133](https://www.merkle.com/en/merkle-now/articles-blogs/2026/from-program-enterprise-platform.html), [152](https://www.scribd.com/document/819588329/Skills)]. Therefore, investment decisions should be framed in terms of this long-term value creation rather than short-term profit margins.

The implementation plan, as referenced in `implentationplanphase.md`, provides a clear roadmap for phased investment [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Phase 4.5, titled "Points Economy," is positioned between Phase 4 (Publishing) and Phase 5 (Orders), indicating its dependency on foundational platform features being in place [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The bulk of the initial investment should be focused on completing Phase 4.5, which encompasses the P0 features essential for a functional loyalty economy. This includes building the verified video-earning pipeline, implementing daily caps and cooldowns, and establishing the basic redemption flow at checkout [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The exit criteria for this phase are stringent, requiring a fully functional system that earns points from a heartbeat-verified video watch, displays a correctly derived wallet balance, and produces a balanced ledger entry upon redemption [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Only after this core loop is proven and stable should investment shift towards Phase 5 and beyond, which involves integrating the `ProductPurchaseWithPoints` flow into the broader order management and accounting systems [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This sequenced approach prevents premature optimization and ensures the system's accounting is mathematically sound before it is exposed to significant commerce traffic. Features designated as P2, such as referral invites (`C-8`), automated expiry reminders (`C-9`), and offline watch queues (`C-15`), represent future investments that can enhance the user experience but are not necessary for the initial launch [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Allocating resources to these later would divert focus from achieving the critical milestones of the core system.

From a unit economic perspective, the cost of the loyalty program is the value of the discounts granted to consumers. This cost is precisely defined by the `TenantPointSettings` table, specifically the `pointsPerCurrencyMinor` field, which dictates the exchange rate from points to the tenant's local currency [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. For example, if a tenant sets this value to 100, it means 100 points are equivalent to one minor unit of currency (e.g., one cent). If a user redeems 2,500 points and the order currency is USD, the direct cost to the tenant is $25.00. However, this direct cost must be weighed against the indirect benefits. The primary benefit is increased CLV. The act of watching a promotional video is a form of engagement that builds brand awareness and affinity, keeping the user active on the platform and more likely to make purchases they might not have otherwise [[18](https://connectpay.com/blog/how-digital-wallets-are-building-customer-loyalty/)]. The goal is to acquire customers whose total spend over their lifetime exceeds the cumulative value of the points they redeem. Another key metric is the cost of rewards versus the incremental sales they generate. Stop wasting budget on points nobody uses; the system is designed to be data-driven, allowing for adjustments to earning rules and redemption thresholds to optimize for engagement and profitability [[27](https://manago.ai/blog/customer-loyalty-program)].

A significant portion of the investment budget must be allocated to fraud prevention, as protecting the economic viability of the loyalty program is paramount. The system incorporates a multi-layered fraud detection strategy, and building and maintaining this infrastructure requires dedicated resources. This includes the backend for verifying heartbeat logs to detect simulated watch time, the device fingerprinting service to identify suspicious devices, the rate-limiting middleware to prevent excessive session starts, and the administrative review queue for handling `FRAUD_SUSPECT` events [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. An integration test that deliberately fabricates a heartbeat log to trigger a `FRAUD_SUSPECT` status and landing in the admin queue is a required acceptance criterion, underscoring its importance [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. Investing in robust anti-fraud measures is not an operational expense but a critical component of the unit economics, as preventing abuse directly protects the margin and ensures the program remains sustainable. The overall investment thesis should be centered on building a defensible moat around customer relationships. By creating a sticky, engaging loyalty loop, OmniSell can differentiate itself from competitors and foster a loyal user base, which is a far more valuable asset than short-term arbitrage opportunities [[26](https://medium.com/@onecaseman/loyalty-marketing-part-i-strategies-and-segments-bbd326d9b130), [95](https://www.cgap.org/research/publication/elements-of-successful-loyalty-model-in-merchant-payments)].

## Customer Acquisition and Retention Strategies

The OmniSell OS points system is engineered with a powerful feedback loop that drives both customer acquisition and long-term retention. The strategy hinges on transforming passive content consumption into an active, rewarding experience, thereby hooking new users and encouraging them to return regularly. This is achieved through a carefully designed acquisition funnel and a suite of retention mechanics embedded directly into the system's architecture. The ultimate goal is to create a habit-forming cycle where users are motivated to earn points through engagement and then spend them to receive tangible value, cementing their loyalty to the platform.

The customer acquisition funnel begins with a seamless sign-up process that automatically enrolls the user as a consumer [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. The critical activation event—the moment a user understands the value of the loyalty system—is the first point they earn. The onboarding flow is therefore designed to guide the user directly to this moment. A prominent prompt, such as "Watch this introductory video and earn your first 50 points," serves as a powerful hook, converting the act of learning about the platform into an immediate reward [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/)]. This tactic is highly effective because it provides instant gratification and clearly communicates the system's core mechanic. Once activated, the user's next logical step is monetization: making their first purchase. The system facilitates this by gating the redemption feature behind a minimum points threshold (`minRedeemPoints`) [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [108](https://developer.eagleeye.com/eagleeye-developer/docs/segment)]. This ensures the user has accumulated enough points to make a meaningful discount, demonstrating the utility of the feature and encouraging their first transaction. Following this, the user enters an expansion phase, where they continue to engage with the platform by watching more videos and exploring products, gradually becoming more proficient and invested in the ecosystem.

Retention is fostered by the system's inherent design, which promotes recurring engagement. The combination of `maxDailyCap` and `cooldownSeconds` on earning rules is a deliberate strategy to encourage users to return to the platform each day to continue accumulating points [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This transforms a one-time action into a daily habit, significantly increasing user retention and time-on-platform. The UI plays a crucial role here by providing transparent feedback on daily progress, such as a meter showing today's earnings versus the daily cap, which taps into behavioral psychology to motivate continued interaction [[29](https://www.quora.com/How-does-audience-segmentation-affect-the-development-of-loyalty-programs), [30](https://scal-e.com/how-to-segment-customers-for-a-customised-loyalty-programme/)]. Another powerful retention tool is the concept of loss aversion, implemented through the `autoExpireDays` setting in `TenantPointSettings` [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. By setting a finite lifespan for points, the system creates a gentle sense of urgency, motivating users to spend their hard-earned rewards before they disappear. This encourages repeat visits and purchases to avoid losing value.

Trust is a cornerstone of long-term retention, and the points system is designed with mechanisms to reinforce it. A particularly important feature is the logic for handling refunds. When a user redeems points for a purchase and later receives a refund, the system does not simply remove the points from their balance. Instead, it creates a new `PointTransaction` of type `EARN` with a source of `"redemption_refund"` to restore the points to the user's wallet [[35](https://www.omnicell.com/resources/video/omnisphere/), [36](https://ir.omnicell.com/news/news-details/2024/Omnicell-Announces-OmniSphere/default.aspx)]. This ensures that points are never silently lost due to unforeseen circumstances like returns or cancellations. This transparent and fair policy builds significant user trust, assuring members that their loyalty and effort are respected and that their rewards are secure. This commitment to fairness is a powerful differentiator and a key factor in retaining users over the long term. The continuous loop of earning points through engagement and spending them for value creates a virtuous cycle that makes the platform increasingly sticky. Users become more familiar with the merchant's offerings, leading to more informed and confident purchases, which in turn reinforces their positive experience and desire to continue participating in the loyalty program.
